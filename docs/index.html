YAMS for Umbrel

Your own media server, in one install.

Jellyfin, Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, Bazarr and qBittorrent, already connected to each other, with downloads locked behind a VPN. Built on YAMS, packaged for umbrelOS.

Community app store address
https://github.com/Deepshah0308/yams-umbrel-store

Install

  1. 1

    Add the store

    In Umbrel, open the App Store, click ⋯ in the top right, choose Community App Stores, paste the address above and click Add.

  2. 2

    Install YAMS

    Open YAMS App Store, click YAMS, then Install. The first install downloads about 2–3 GB, so give it a few minutes.

  3. 3

    Open YAMS and follow its four steps

    Paste your VPN's WireGuard file, pick your indexers in Prowlarr, add a show or movie, then start watching in Jellyfin at http://umbrel.local:8097.

What you get

  • JellyfinWatch on your TV, phone, console or browser.
  • SonarrFollows your shows and grabs new episodes.
  • RadarrFinds and organises movies.
  • ProwlarrOne place to choose where YAMS searches, with FlareSolverr for Cloudflare.
  • BazarrSubtitles, automatically.
  • qBittorrentDownloads only through your VPN, and stops if the VPN drops.

The YAMS dashboard runs the whole stack: status and logs for every app, a VPN leak check, one-click fixes, backups, and updates to the newest version of every app whenever you like. Two widgets put VPN status and downloads on your Umbrel home screen.

Questions

Which VPNs work?

Any VPN that lets you download a WireGuard configuration file, such as Proton VPN, Mullvad or AirVPN. YAMS fixes the parts of the file that trip people up, and downloads stay off until the VPN is connected.

Where are my movies and shows?

In Umbrel's Files app under Downloads → media. Downloads in progress are in Downloads → torrents.

How do I update?

Open YAMS, go to Updates and click Update all apps, or switch on weekly automatic updates. YAMS backs up your settings first. New YAMS features arrive through Umbrel's own Update button.

Something isn't working.

Try Quick fixes → Fix everything in the YAMS dashboard. Every app also has a Logs button. If you're still stuck, open an issue and paste the logs.

Can I move my setup to another Umbrel?

Yes. Make a backup in the YAMS dashboard, install YAMS on the new Umbrel, upload the backup and click Restore.

Help make it better
